## Description
Phoenix Care is the companion app for clinics running Phoenix OS.

• Sign in with a one-time email code  
• Scan a clinic QR or enter your invite to link pets  
• See pets from every Phoenix clinic you’re connected to  
• Request, reschedule, or cancel appointments  
• Live care journey when your pet is in clinic  
• Medications & vaccination records from visit history  
• Message your clinic and get in-app notifications  
• Clinic staff can open the full Phoenix OS dashboard in-app  

Shop and loyalty rewards are coming soon.

Powered by Phoenix OS.
